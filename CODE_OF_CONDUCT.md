# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss the code, data and decisions under review. Give people room to ask questions and disagree. Respect privacy and permission limits when sharing recordings, reports or contact details.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable in project spaces. Report incidents privately to a repository maintainer through an available private contact channel. Include links or timestamps where possible. If the incident involves that maintainer, contact another maintainer when available or report it through GitHub's abuse tools.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict participation in response to an incident. They should limit access to details, communicate the resolution privately where practical and apply decisions consistently.

In reports about the desktop, remove private filenames and file paths from screenshots and logs before sharing them.
